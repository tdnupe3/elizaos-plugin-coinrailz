**Additional 0.1% commission on every referral's first qualifying transaction**

- **$100 transaction**: $0.30 commission ($0.40 for first transaction)

## Implementation Guidelines

### A/B Testing Recommendations
1. **Commission Focus vs. Sustainability Focus**: Test whether users respond better to "earn commissions" or "sustainable income"
2. **Tier Emphasis**: Test highlighting highest tier (0.6%) vs. showing full range
3. **Bonus Emphasis**: Test promoting first transaction bonus vs. volume scaling

- **Week 2**: First transaction bonuses

## Compliance Notes

- All commission rates are clearly stated
- No misleading "unlimited earning" claims
- Transparent about minimum transaction requirements
- Commission caps clearly disclosed
- Sustainability messaging backed by business model analysis

**Key Message**: "Profitable for you, sustainable for the platform, fair for everyone."
