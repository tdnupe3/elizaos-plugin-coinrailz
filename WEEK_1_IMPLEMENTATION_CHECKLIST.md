### TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

#### Common Setup Issues
**Clay Data Import Errors**
- Verify CSV format matches Clay template
- Check for special characters in company names
- Ensure email addresses are valid format

**Apollo Email Authentication**
- Set up SPF record with hosting provider
- Configure DKIM authentication
- Test email deliverability with mail-tester.com

**LinkedIn Connection Limits**
- Stay under 20 connection requests per day
- Personalize every connection request
- Wait 2-3 days between follow-ups

#### Performance Optimization
**Low Email Open Rates**
- A/B test subject lines with ChatGPT
- Check sender reputation and deliverability
- Verify prospect email accuracy in Clay

**Poor LinkedIn Response**
- Increase personalization in connection requests
- Reference recent company news or achievements
- Focus on value proposition, not sales pitch
