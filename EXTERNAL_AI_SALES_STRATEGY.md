#### Day 5-7: Content Creation with ChatGPT
```
ChatGPT Prompts for Outreach:

1. Research Prompt:
"I need to research [Company Name] in the [Industry] sector for a crypto data analytics sales approach. Please provide: key decision makers likely titles, current data challenges they face, competitive pressures, and personalized value proposition for behavioral crypto data. Focus on how multi-chain transaction intelligence could solve their specific problems."

2. Email Generation Prompt:
"Write a professional cold email for [Contact Name], [Title] at [Company]. Their challenges: [Pain Points]. Our solution: Multi-chain behavioral data analytics providing competitive intelligence across 15+ blockchain networks. Include specific benefits for their role, social proof, and clear meeting CTA. Keep under 150 words."

3. LinkedIn Message Prompt:
"Create a LinkedIn connection request message for [Contact Name] at [Company]. Mention their recent [Company News/Achievement]. Introduce crypto behavioral analytics value briefly. Professional tone, under 300 characters."

4. Follow-up Sequence Prompt:
"Create a 5-email follow-up sequence for unresponsive prospects. Each email should provide unique value: competitive insight, market trend, case study, limited offer, final outreach. Professional, not pushy. Include 3-day gaps between emails."
```
