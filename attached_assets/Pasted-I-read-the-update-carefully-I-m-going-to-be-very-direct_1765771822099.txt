The One Thing I’d Tighten (Important but Minor)
Normalize the units very explicitly

You included:

requiredAmountUsd: "$0.75"

That’s good, but you should also include the token decimals and denomination explicitly, e.g.:

"tokenDecimals": 6,
"requiredAmountRaw": "750000",
"requiredAmountHuman": "0.75"


This prevents:

confusion between USDC (6 decimals) and USDT (6, but future tokens may differ)

downstream math bugs

It’s a small addition that makes the response future-proof.
