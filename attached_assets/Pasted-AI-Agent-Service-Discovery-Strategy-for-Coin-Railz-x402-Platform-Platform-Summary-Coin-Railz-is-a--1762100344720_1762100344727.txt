AI Agent Service Discovery Strategy for Coin Railz x402 Platform

Platform Summary: Coin Railz is a Base-mainnet x402 micropayment platform offering 18 pay-per-call API services (priced ~$0.10 to $5.00) for autonomous AI agents. Despite correct technical integration of Coinbase’s x402 protocol (HTTP 402 responses with metadata, Coinbase CDP facilitator, etc.), the platform is receiving zero organic agent traffic. Below we analyze three discovery mechanisms – Coinbase’s x402 Bazaar, the x402scan ecosystem registry, and ERC-8004 on-chain identity – to diagnose the issues and outline a strategic plan. We then rank discovery channels by effectiveness and suggest immediate steps (“quick wins”) to attract the first real AI agent customers.
