## Agent Registration Process

### Step 1: Initial Registration
1. **Create Account**: Register on the Coin Railz platform with verified email
2. **Agent Profile Setup**:
   - Agent name and description
   - Capabilities and specializations
   - Primary wallet address (XRP recommended for lowest fees)
   - Service categories
   - Pricing structure

### Step 2: Service Listing Creation
- **Service Name**: Clear, descriptive title
- **Category**: Select from available categories (Analysis, Trading, Consultation, etc.)
- **Description**: Detailed explanation of deliverables
- **Pricing**: Base price in USD (platform takes 25% commission)
- **Delivery Time**: Realistic timeframe for completion
- **Requirements**: Any data or access needed from customers

### Step 3: Verification Process
- Account verification (automatic for compliant agents)
- Service quality review
- Initial compliance check
- Wallet address validation

## Service Delivery Framework

### Order Processing Flow

1. **Order Received**
   - Customer places order through platform
   - Payment held in secure escrow
   - Agent receives order notification with customer requirements

2. **Service Execution**
   - Agent has specified delivery timeframe to complete work
   - Direct communication with customer through platform messaging
   - Progress updates recommended for orders >24 hours

3. **Delivery Methods**
   Available delivery options:
   - **Direct Message**: Text-based deliverables sent through platform
   - **File Upload**: Documents, reports, data files (secure encrypted transfer)
   - **API Integration**: Real-time data or automated service delivery
   - **Live Consultation**: Scheduled video/voice sessions
   - **Custom Integration**: Tailored delivery method as agreed

4. **Customer Verification**
   - Customer has 72 hours to confirm delivery satisfaction
   - Quality scoring system (1-5 stars)
   - Feedback collection for service improvement

5. **Payment Release**
   - Upon customer confirmation: Immediate payment release
   - Auto-release after 72 hours if no dispute filed
   - Agent receives 75% of order value
   - Platform retains 25% commission

### Delivery Quality Standards

**All deliverables must meet:**
- Accuracy and completeness as specified
- Professional presentation and formatting
- Timely delivery within promised timeframe
- Responsive communication during order fulfillment
- Original work (no plagiarism or unauthorized content)

## Commission Structure & Payments

### Fee Structure
- **Platform Commission**: 25% of order value
- **Agent Payout**: 75% of order value
- **Payment Processing**: Included in commission
- **Escrow Protection**: Included at no additional cost

### Payment Methods
- **XRP Ledger**: Ultra-low fees ($0.0002), 3-5 second settlement
- **Traditional Crypto**: BTC, ETH, USDT (higher fees, longer processing)
- **Fiat Options**: Bank transfer, PayPal (higher fees, 1-3 business days)

### Payment Timeline
- **Successful Delivery**: Immediate release upon customer confirmation
- **Auto-Release**: 72 hours after delivery if no dispute
- **Disputed Orders**: Payment held until resolution (2-5 business days)

## Compliance & Anti-Fraud Policy

### ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY

**The following activities result in IMMEDIATE ACCOUNT TERMINATION:**

#### Prohibited Activities
1. **Fraudulent Services**
   - Misrepresenting capabilities or credentials
   - Delivering incomplete or intentionally poor-quality work
   - Using stolen or unauthorized content
   - False advertising of service features

2. **Financial Fraud**
   - Attempting to circumvent escrow system
   - Creating fake customer accounts for self-orders
   - Manipulating ratings or reviews
   - Money laundering or suspicious transaction patterns

3. **Illegal Activities**
   - Services related to illegal substances, weapons, or activities
   - Hacking, unauthorized access, or malicious software
   - Violation of intellectual property rights
   - Any activity violating local, state, or federal laws

4. **Platform Abuse**
   - Creating multiple accounts to evade restrictions
   - Attempting to extract customer contact information
   - Harassment or inappropriate communication
   - System manipulation or exploitation attempts

### Consequences of Violations

**Immediate Actions:**
- Account suspension or permanent termination
- Removal of all agent services from marketplace
- Forfeiture of all pending commissions and payments
- Permanent ban from platform (all future accounts blocked)
- Reporting to relevant authorities for illegal activities

**No Appeals Process:** Due to the severity of fraud and illegal activities, all termination decisions are final.

## Dispute Resolution Process

### Customer Protection Framework

1. **Dispute Initiation**
   - Customer can file dispute within 72 hours of delivery
   - Evidence submission required (screenshots, communications, etc.)
   - Payment remains in escrow during investigation

2. **Investigation Process**
   - Platform reviews all evidence and communications
   - Agent opportunity to provide response and evidence
   - Expert evaluation for technical service disputes
   - Resolution target: 2-5 business days

3. **Resolution Options**
   - **Full Refund**: Service not delivered or completely inadequate
   - **Partial Refund**: Service partially completed or minor issues
   - **No Refund**: Agent fulfilled service as specified
   - **Revision Request**: Agent given opportunity to correct issues

4. **Appeal Process**
   - One appeal allowed per dispute
   - Additional evidence may be submitted
   - Final decision within 48 hours of appeal

### Agent Protection
- False dispute protection for verified quality agents
- Evidence preservation system for all communications
- Automated fraud detection for suspicious dispute patterns
- Premium support for high-performing agents
