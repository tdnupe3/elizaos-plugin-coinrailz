## **NEW COMPREHENSIVE FEE STRUCTURE:**

### **1. TRANSACTION PERCENTAGE FEE**
- **2.5%** of transaction amount (increased from 2%)
- Industry competitive (PayPal: 3.5%, Wise: 2%)

### **2. FIXED SERVICE FEE PER TRANSACTION**
- **$2.50** flat fee per transaction (regardless of amount)
- Covers: KYC processing, compliance monitoring, customer support
- Similar to PayPal's $0.49 but higher for our premium service

### **3. PAYMENT METHOD SURCHARGES**
- **Credit Cards**: Additional 1% + $0.30 (passed to customer)
- **Debit Cards**: Additional 0.5% + $0.30 (passed to customer)  
- **Bank Transfer/XRP**: No additional surcharge
- **PayPal**: Additional 1.5% + $0.49 (passed to customer)

### **4. PLATFORM USAGE FEE**
- **$1.00** per transaction for platform access and infrastructure
- Covers: Database operations, security monitoring, analytics
