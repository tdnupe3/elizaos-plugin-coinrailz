```
                Travis Kellogg
```

**Key structural points**:

- Coin Railz LLC and Kellogg Holdings LLC are **separate, independent entities**
- KHLLC does **not** own Coin Railz LLC — there is no parent-subsidiary relationship
- Both entities share a common founder (Travis Kellogg)
- KHLLC provides regulatory backbone services to Coin Railz where applicable (money transmission compliance under the Alabama MTL)
- Each entity maintains its own EIN, bank accounts, and books
- The SAFE investment is in Coin Railz LLC only

### 1.4 Regulatory Services Relationship

Where money transmission licensing is required for Coin Railz operations, KHLLC provides regulatory coverage through its Alabama MTL. This arrangement:

- Allows Coin Railz to operate with compliance-first positioning without obtaining its own MTL
- Provides a pathway to independent licensing as Coin Railz scales
- Leverages KHLLC's established principal-to-principal digital asset settlement experience and U.S. banking relationships
