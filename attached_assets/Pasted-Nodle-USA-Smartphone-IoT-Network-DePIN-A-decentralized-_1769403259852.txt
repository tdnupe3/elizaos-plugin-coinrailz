Nodle (USA – Smartphone IoT Network DePIN): A decentralized IoT network that uses millions of smartphones as connectivity nodes for BLE (Bluetooth) devices. Often described as “Uber for IoT,” Nodle pays users (“Nodle Cash”) for the data their phones relay.

Data: Sensor and tag data via Bluetooth – e.g., asset trackers, beacons, environmental sensors. Nodle’s network provides location and connectivity for these devices using phones as the link. They also gather “digital proof of location” and have introduced products for content authentication (e.g., ContentSign for media), expanding into a broader digital trust network.

Contact: Nodle’s site has a “Contact us” page (likely a form). Additionally, developer queries can go to support@nodle.com. The Nodle team is active on Discord and other social channels for community collaborations.

Developer Program: Yes – Nodle offers an SDK (Nodle SDK) for app developers to integrate Nodle and earn revenue by joining the network. They also have Nodle Connect for enterprise integration of Bluetooth asset tracking. With the recent migration to new blockchains (moving from Parity Substrate to Solana/ZKsync), they are updating docs and encouraging devs to build on the network for new use cases (e.g. locating stolen items, verifying IoT data).

Openness to Integration: Very high. Nodle actively seeks partnerships in both Web3 and traditional IoT. For example, they’ve partnered with governments for COVID sensor tracking and with apps to integrate the Nodle earning mechanism. Nodle’s multi-chain approach and existing micropayment (NODL token) system for data means a Coin Railz pilot enabling multi-chain USDC payments for IoT data payloads is conceptually right in their wheelhouse. The Nodle Foundation could fast-track a pilot as they look to drive adoption and real-world utility in the near term.
