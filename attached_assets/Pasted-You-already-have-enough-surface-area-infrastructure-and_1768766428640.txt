2️⃣ Publish an MQTT Monetization Proxy (this is your wedge)

This is the single most important thing you can do.

What it is

A lightweight service (or docker image) that sits between:

IoT Device → MQTT Broker


and adds:

metering

payment enforcement

credits / USDC billing

Why this works

MQTT is still dominant in IoT (this is current, not old)

No one wants to touch payments inside firmware

A proxy = zero device changes

What to ship (MVP)

Docker image:

coinrailz/mqtt-paywall


Config:

pricing:
  per_message: 0.001
payment:
  method: credits | usdc


Behavior:

message arrives

proxy checks balance

if paid → forward

if not → reject or queue

This immediately enables:

pay-per-sensor

data marketplaces

DePIN billing

enterprise pilots

This is way more powerful than research.
