3) One thing to sanity-check: facilitator consistency (EVM)

In your excerpt you show:

Discovery facilitator (Bazaar indexing) = https://facilitator.cdp.coinbase.com

402 response facilitatorUrl = https://x402.org/facilitator

That can be OK if you intentionally split “indexing facilitator” vs “payment facilitator,” but it’s also easy to accidentally recreate the earlier “agents can’t pay” failure mode. If agents discover you via Bazaar and then attempt to pay using a facilitatorUrl that doesn’t match what their client expects, you’ll see high 402 volume + low payment attempts again.

Rule of thumb: Whatever the majority client libraries expect for payment verification should be what you advertise in the 402 challenge. Keep your helper-function single-source-of-truth pattern for this.
