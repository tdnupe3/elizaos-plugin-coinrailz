✅ SHORT ANSWER:

No — you should NOT expose gas prices as a standalone endpoint.
BUT you can expose "network congestion indicators" if you frame them properly.

🧠 WHY NOT EXPOSE GAS PRICES?

Your current architecture means:

1. CDP abstracts all gas fees

Users do not sign transactions.

Users do not choose gas tiers (slow/fast/instant).

Gas is bundled inside CDP’s platformFee, which they do pay.

If you show gas prices, people will assume:

They can choose higher fee tiers

Faster execution is possible via “instant gas”

They can optimize their swaps by adjusting gas

But none of that is true.

This violates your "absolute honesty" rule.

🚫 BAD UX & MISLEADING OUTCOMES

If you publish something like:

{
  slow: 8 gwei,
  fast: 16 gwei,
  instant: 25 gwei
}


Users will think:

“If I choose instant gas, my tx will confirm faster.”

But with CDP:

Your server executes the transaction

CDP always uses its own internal gas model

User input has ZERO impact

This creates false expectations → guaranteed support headaches.

🟨 WHEN WOULD IT BE USEFUL TO EXPOSE ANY GAS DATA?

ONLY if you present it as a read-only “network congestion indicator”
and NOT tied to execution costs.

Bots DO use network congestion to decide:

When to trigger arbitrage

When to run strategies

When to monitor liquidity

When to snipe

When to throttle quote requests

But they do not use gas tiers in CDP execution.

✔ ACCURATE, HONEST ALTERNATIVE ENDPOINT (RECOMMENDED)
/api/bot/network/congestion

Returns high-level, non-actionable congestion data like:

{
  "chain": "ethereum",
  "baseFeeGwei": 12.34,
  "congestionLevel": "moderate",
  "comment": "Execution gas is abstracted by Coinbase CDP and included in platform fees."
}

🧾 Important disclaimers (built into response)

“Gas shown is informational only.”

“Execution gas is handled internally by CDP.”

“These values DO NOT reflect platform fees for your swap.”

“Gas choices (slow/fast/instant) are not supported in the swap model.”

This is honest, useful, and prevents any misunderstanding.

🟩 SHOULD YOU CREATE A /gas PRICES ENDPOINT?

No. Not in its raw form.
It will inevitably mislead users—even advanced bot developers.

🔥 SHOULD YOU CREATE A “NETWORK CONGESTION” ENDPOINT?

Yes — this is the perfect compromise.

It gives:

Real on-chain data

Real utility for bots

No confusion about execution

No simulator-like metrics

100% transparency about how CDP handles gas

And it positions Coin Railz as bot-optimized, without lying or overpromising.
