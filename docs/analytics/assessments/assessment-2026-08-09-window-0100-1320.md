1. **Add per-IP + per-subnet unpaid POST rate limiting (429 + exponential backoff)** — Target: 74.220.48.55 (178 POSTs), IPv6 burst cycles (69 POSTs). Every unpaid POST currently generates a DB write. Architect confirmed the existing rate limiter is keyed on payment address first, then IP — unauthenticated actors are not limited by POST volume. Add a per-IP budget for unpaid POST attempts (e.g. 20/hour, soft 429 with `Retry-After`) while preserving higher limits for wallets with any payment history.

Confirm that when an MCP `tools/call` hits a 402, the client receives an actionable payment recipe (price, network, facilitator, wallet). If not, this is a silent conversion blocker for MCP-native agents.

## 10. Final Verdict

**High-discovery, no-conversion window. Platform is technically healthy, commercially stuck.**

The infrastructure is clean — rail fired twice, all DB inserts succeeded, no broken routes, no security breaches. Discovery is broad: 84 unique IPs on agent.json in 12 hours, three MCP registries indexing the tool surface, meta-externalagent continuing its consistent catalog sweep, SmartFlowPro running its 47-service check.

The story is in the details: the earthdata payer probed three new services without paying (evaluation phase), 74.220.48.55 pivoted from depleted-wallet retries to a full catalog sweep (unknown catalyst), and the IPv6 agent picked up MCP in addition to x402 (multi-protocol integration research). All three are moving in directions that could precede a payment. None of them paid.

The conversion problem is structural. 817 intents, $327.54, 16 wallets across the platform's entire history tells you demand exists but is thin and concentrated. The named follow-ups (SmartFlowPro, Hermes) are the most actionable near-term revenue levers. Everything else is observation.

**Confidence: High** on traffic characterization, actor classification, and technical status.
**Medium** on 74.220.48.55 being a genuine evaluation pivot (could still be a different agent on the same egress).
**Medium** on earthdata payer returning to pay earthdata-granules (pattern supports it; not guaranteed).
**High** on earthdata-granules as the next most-likely conversion endpoint given three-actor convergence this window.
