Helium Network (USA/global – Decentralized Wireless): The largest decentralized wireless IoT network, known as “The People’s Network.” Helium uses community-hosted hotspots to provide LoRaWAN (IoT) and 5G cellular coverage, and rewards hosts in HNT tokens.

Data: IoT device connectivity data – Helium carries sensor data (location pings, meter readings, etc.) for millions of IoT devices. Users (enterprises or hobbyists) purchase Data Credits (pegged to USD) to send data over the network. Essentially it monetizes network usage per packet.

Contact: Helium encourages business use via its website: “Contact us to bring Helium to your business.”. Interested companies can also engage via the Helium Foundation (steward of the network) or Nova Labs (the company managing commercial efforts). The Helium Foundation site provides grants and has community channels for support.

Developer Program: Open and thriving – Helium provides extensive documentation and open-source tools. There’s no gatekeeping: any developer can use the network by buying Data Credits. Helium has a console for IoT device onboarding and an API for network stats. They also run a Grants Program to fund integrations.
