Two actionable nits 🛠️

Price/amount mismatch
Your route config says price: "$0.10", but the 402 shows "amount": "10000".
On Base USDC (6 decimals), $0.10 = 100000 atomic units, not 10000 (which is $0.01).

If you set price: "$0.10" and let the middleware compute the atomic amount, the 402 should show amount: "100000".

If you’re overriding amounts manually anywhere, update to "100000".

Why this matters: The facilitator/Bazaar expect coherent pricing. A mismatch won’t always “break” ingestion, but it’s a subtle, avoidable edge that can cause silent rejects.
