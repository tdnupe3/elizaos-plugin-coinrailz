## BUSINESS MODEL & REVENUE STREAMS

### Revenue Sources

**1. Transaction Fees** (Projected 60% of revenue):
- Person-to-person transfers: $5 base fee + 3% of transaction amount
- Cryptocurrency trading: 0.75% per transaction
- International payments: Competitive rates with premium for speed

**2. AI Marketplace Commissions** (Projected 25% of revenue):
- Commission on AI service transactions: 3.5% platform fee
- Monthly subscription fees for premium AI services: $50-$500/month
- Enterprise AI licensing: $1,000-$10,000/month

**3. Subscription Services** (Projected 10% of revenue):
- Premium account features: $9.99-$29.99/month
- Business accounts: $99-$499/month
- Enterprise solutions: $1,000+/month

**4. Data Analytics** (Projected 5% of revenue):
- Market intelligence reports: $500-$5,000 per report
- Custom analytics: $10,000-$50,000 per project
- API access for financial data: Usage-based pricing

### Revenue Model Advantages
- **Recurring Revenue**: 35% from subscriptions creates predictable income
- **Scalable**: Digital services with high profit margins
- **Diversified**: Multiple revenue streams reduce dependency risk
- **Network Effects**: Growth accelerates as platform expands
