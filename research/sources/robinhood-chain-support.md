Robinhood Chain

Robinhood Chain is a permissionless, Ethereum-compatible, Layer-2 blockchain built to bring traditional markets, crypto, and real-world assets together on a fast, efficient, and open network.

Key points:

- **Open and permissionless**: anyone can interact with the network, build applications, and deploy smart contracts
- **Built for real-world assets**: Robinhood Chain is designed for tokenized real-world assets including Stock Tokens and ETFs that can be held, transferred, and composed into applications onchain
- **Ethereum-compatible**: Robinhood Chain is fully EVM-compatible, so any wallet or dapp that supports standard Ethereum tooling can connect directly
- **Supported by Robinhood Wallet**: Robinhood Wallet users can connect to Robinhood Chain to send, receive, and swap crypto, and interact with dapps
- **Uses ETH for gas**: transaction fees on Robinhood Chain are paid in ETH, the same as on Ethereum
- **Not connected to your Robinhood brokerage or crypto accounts**: Robinhood Chain operates independently of the main Robinhood app and doesn't affect your investments, crypto balances, or portfolio

## FAQ

[**FAQ**](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-chain-mainnet/#FAQ)

What is the Robinhood Chain?

Robinhood Chain is a permissionless, Ethereum-compatible Layer-2 blockchain built to support a new era of onchain financial infrastructure bringing traditional markets, crypto, and real-world assets together on a fast, efficient, and open network.

How do I connect to Robinhood Chain?

Robinhood Wallet natively supports Robinhood Chain with no manual setup required. You can also connect using any EVM-compatible wallet by adding the network manually with the following details:

- **Network name**: Robinhood Chain
- **Chain ID**: 4663
- **RPC URL**: [https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com](https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com/)
- **Currency symbol**: ETH
- **Block explorer**: robinhoodchain.blockscout.com

How do I move funds onto Robinhood Chain?

You can bridge ETH and supported ERC-20 tokens to Robinhood Chain using the canonical Arbitrum bridge or partner bridging routes. See the [Robinhood Chain developer documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/) for all options and supported assets.

How do I deploy an app on Robinhood Chain?

Robinhood Chain is fully EVM-compatibleCheck out the Robinhood Chain developer documentation to get started.

Is the testnet still available?

Yes. The Robinhood Chain testnet remains available for development and testing purposes. Testnet tokens have no real-world value and are only for development use. You can get testnet tokens at [faucet.testnet.chain.robinhood.com](http://faucet.testnet.chain.robinhood.com/).

Where can I submit feedback?

You can share feedback by sending us an email at [chain-developers-group@robinhood.com](mailto:chain-developers-group@robinhood.com).
