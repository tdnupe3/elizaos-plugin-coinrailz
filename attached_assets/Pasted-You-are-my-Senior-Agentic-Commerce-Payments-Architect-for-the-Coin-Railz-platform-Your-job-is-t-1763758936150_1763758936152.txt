SECTION 2 – WHAT GOOGLE AP2 IS (YOUR WORKING MODEL)
========================

Use the latest public docs/blogs/specs for Google’s “Agent Payments Protocol (AP2)” as your source of truth.

My understanding (which you should refine):

- AP2 is an open, payment-agnostic protocol for **AI-agent initiated payments**, built as an extension on top of:
  - Agent2Agent (A2A) – a protocol for agent-to-agent communication.
  - Model Context Protocol (MCP) – a protocol for tools/resources in LLM ecosystems.
- AP2 focuses on the **authorization & traceability layer** of payments, not one specific rail.
- The core concept is **mandates**:
  - Intent Mandate – expresses what the user authorizes the agent to do (constraints like merchant categories, price caps, time limits, etc.).
  - Cart Mandate – describes the specific items/cart the agent has assembled and that the user has approved.
  - Payment Mandate – expresses the final “go execute this payment” authorization, with all relevant evidence.
- AP2 emphasizes:
  - Cryptographically-signed, verifiable objects.
  - An auditable trail that can answer: “What did the user authorize? What did the agent actually do? Who’s liable when something goes wrong?”
  - Compatibility with different rails (cards, bank transfers, stablecoins, x402, etc.).

When you design, assume that AP2 mandates + evidence will be represented as structured JSON objects that can be signed, stored, and passed between services.
