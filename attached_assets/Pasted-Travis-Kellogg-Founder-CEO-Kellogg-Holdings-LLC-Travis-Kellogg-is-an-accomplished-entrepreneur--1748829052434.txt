Travis Kellogg – Founder & CEO, Kellogg Holdings LLC
Travis Kellogg is an accomplished entrepreneur, fintech innovator, and business development consultant with a proven track record of building and scaling technology-driven ventures. As the Founder and CEO of Kellogg Holdings LLC, Travis leads a multifaceted company specializing in money transmission, payment solutions, emerging technologies, and strategic consulting.

A recognized expert at the intersection of finance, blockchain, and artificial intelligence, Travis has guided Kellogg Holdings LLC to become a licensed money transmitter in Alabama, demonstrating full compliance with regulatory standards and an unwavering commitment to ethical business practices. Under his leadership, the firm delivers secure, compliant domestic and international money movement, paymaster services, currency exchange, and cutting-edge P2P payment and digital asset solutions through its flagship platform, Coin Railz.

Travis brings extensive experience in AI prompt engineering, smart contract development, OTC trading, liquidity provision, and corporate structuring. He has played a key role in launching and supporting token projects, developing robust AML/KYC compliance frameworks, and implementing advanced risk management strategies for digital assets.

He is also a respected thought leader, having delivered presentations at academic institutions such as Tennessee State University and major industry events like the Bitcoin Conference in Nashville, where he shared insights on the future of fintech, crypto market trends, and the potential of decentralized finance.

Prior to founding Kellogg Holdings, Travis built a reputation for excellence in business development, client relationship management, and technology integration, with a focus on sustainable growth and digital transformation. He holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) with a concentration in Marketing from Clark Atlanta University and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance from Stillman College.
