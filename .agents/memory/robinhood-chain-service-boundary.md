---
name: Robinhood Chain service boundary
description: Product-claim boundary for Robinhood Chain liquidity and Stock Token intelligence.
---

Do not describe query-limited discovery data as chain-wide liquidity, and do not describe raw price-feed reads as production-grade Stock Token intelligence. Those claims require canonical pool indexing and the oracle, sequencer, multiplier, pause, and corporate-action controls documented by Robinhood.

**Why:** Robinhood Chain's RWA activity creates a strong opportunity, but incomplete pool discovery and raw feed reads can turn technically valid responses into materially misleading market or asset claims.

**How to apply:** Use explicit coverage and provenance fields until canonical indexing is live. Treat Stock Token price, eligibility, transferability, and liquidity as separate facts, and combine official metadata and corporate actions with validated on-chain state before marketing the service as decision-grade.
