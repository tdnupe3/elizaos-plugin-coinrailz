i. 	A full accounting of all open invoices shall be performed and all open invoices must be paid current;
ii. 	The appropriate deposit referenced in this section must be paid in full.

6.4.  Supplier Deposits.  Due to the real-time nature of funds collections in 6.2, CLIENT deposits are minimized.  Actual deposit will be 15 days average supplier costs which will be due upon the acquisition of 100 customers.  Once 1,000 customers have been acquired, UNIFIED SIGNAL will calculate the daily average supplier costs for those 1,000 customers and multiply that by CLIENT’s ending subscriber forecast for the next month to calculate the required deposit.  Deposits will grow based on actual subscriber growth. 

Mobile Wallet Offload Prefunding Account Deposits.  Due to the real-time nature of funds clearing from the mobile wallet to the debit card and mobile wallet to other mobile wallet offload suppliers such as (Zelle, MCN, Push to Card, Bank Offload and Private Label Debit Card) “Offload Suppliers”, CLIENT is responsible for having adequate funds in UNIFIED SIGNAL’s supplier prefunding accounts.  Prefunding account deposits need to be a minimum of 5 days of total amount of withdrawals from CLIENT’s customer’s mobile wallet accounts to Offload Suppliers.  UNIFIED SIGNAL will calculate the daily average money moved from CLIENT’s customer’s mobile wallet accounts to Offload Suppliers and multiply that by 5.  Deposits will grow based on actual subscriber growth.

For clarity, open loop describes a method of processing certain point-of-sale payment transactions through open loop payment rails, such as with Visa / MasterCard, while closed loop refers to a method of processing the same transaction via a closed loop payment network, such as through our proprietary mobile wallet platform.  This technology shall be controlled by our internal processing gateway in conjunction with implementation of other key processing elements, with payment transactions typically defaulting to closed loop (mobile wallet), and rolling over to open loop (MasterCard) only when customer funds on the wallet are insufficient to complete the desired transaction.
