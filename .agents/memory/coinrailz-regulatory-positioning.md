---
name: Coin Railz regulatory positioning
description: Boundaries for licensing, bank-settlement, and Circle infrastructure claims in public materials.
---

Coin Railz must not be described as using or operating under the owner’s unrelated money transmitter license. Do not claim that Coin Railz provides regulated money transmission or bank settlement. Describe Circle’s current role only as USDC being a supported payment asset; do not claim Circle Developer-Controlled Wallets, CCTP, EURC, or Circle as core infrastructure unless a live integration is separately verified.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed that the MTL is not used for any Coin Railz activity, and Circle requested clarification after directory copy overstated Circle’s role and implied licensing or settlement capabilities.

**How to apply:** Enforce this boundary in directory profiles, applications, marketing, pitch materials, partner outreach, and compliance descriptions. Current wallet tooling should be described according to the live product rather than planned or legacy Circle work.
